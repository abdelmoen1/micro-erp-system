## 1. The service
Hisbah is shop bookkeeping software: sales, stock, customer debts, suppliers and purchases, through
the Android app and the website. By creating an account or using Hisbah you accept these terms.

## 2. Accounts and roles
- A **store owner** creates the store and is responsible for it, for the staff they invite, and for
  everything recorded under the store.
- **Managers and employees** act for the store with the permissions the owner gives; they must keep
  their password private.
- One account per mobile number. You must give accurate information.

## 3. Free trial
Each new store gets **one free trial of 14 days**, with no payment method required. One trial per
owner mobile number: deleting a store and registering again does not start a new trial. We may end
