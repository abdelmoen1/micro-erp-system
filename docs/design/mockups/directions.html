Hisbah — two visual directions

PROPOSAL — DR-2 part 1  US-1506. The same two screens (login and the POS cart) in two directions, built from the same structure as the approved-for-review low-fi and the same tokens structure. Pick one direction, or ask for a revision. Nothing here is implemented in the app until DR-2 sign-off.

Shared and fixed in both: RTL Arabic first; money in Western digits (UI-D1), tabular, ₪ + 2 decimals, isolated LTR; status = colour + icon + label; touch targets ≥ 48 dp; one primary action per screen; AA contrast on every pair (contrast report). The typeface is independent of the direction (UI-D2): each direction shows one candidate.

A · دفتر — Ledger

Warm paper, ink and the brand teal. It borrows the shopkeeper's debt notebook: hairline ledger rules, the calligraphic wordmark, and the accountant's double rule above the total. Calm and trustworthy; it reads like a record.

Against the design principles (14 §5.2)

  • Speed: scan is a wide target at the top; the checkout button is in the thumb zone.
  • Money is the hero: 34 sp total above a double rule, the accounting sign for "final amount".

المزيد

Against the design principles (14 §5.2)

  • Speed: the largest scan target of the two (76 dp); the last-scanned line is outlined so repeat scans are visible at a glance.
  • Money is the hero: 38 sp white total on navy, the strongest contrast on the screen, like a till display.
  • Meaning, not decoration: navy is structure (top and bottom chrome), teal is action, colours are only for status.
  • Brand: the app icon and the inverted wordmark on the brand navy #180D39.

Trade-offs

  • Heavier; two dark bars reduce the visible list height by about one row.
  • Cards per line are allowed here (one per cart line), but the dashboards must not become card grids (anti-pattern).
